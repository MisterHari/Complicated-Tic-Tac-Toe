Rules
• 3 in a row on any face wins.
• Control 2 opposite physical corners to win.
• After playing on a face, that face is locked for your next 5 moves .
• After playing a corner, your next move must be non-corner .
• Cooldowns are independent for X and O.
• Drag the cube to rotate it.
